# Make the app look like your event

From your event's brand to an app that shows it, event by event and language by language.

**The result:** Each event shows your colours, fonts, home screen and pages, the app's texts carry your wording, and installed apps pick it all up with no new build.

Sources: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/), [Home screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/), [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/).

_App Studio Playbook v1.0.1, built from the App Studio guide v1.1.0, checked against App Studio c1421d4 on 14 Oct 2026. Each step links to the App Studio guide pages it comes from._

## Who does what

- **Your move**: You set the look in App Studio's Look sections: Colours & fonts, Home screen, Custom pages and Translations.
- **Our team is on it**: Your ExpoPlatform team sets what App Studio leaves to them, such as the design style, the tab bar style and the languages Translations offers.

Sources: [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).

## Before you start, have ready

- Your brand colours as #RRGGBB codes, for light and for dark.
- Pictures as PNG or JPEG files of up to 10 MB, each side at least 200 px: the sign-in pictures, the loading backdrop, the home's header picture and your logo.
- A header video if you want one: an MP4 of up to 50 MB.
- The words for the top of each event's home, in English first, then in DE, FR, ES, IT or RU as you need.
- Each page's title and text, in English first.
- The app texts you want in your own words, for each language.

Sources: [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/), [How to change the sign-in pictures and the loading backdrop](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-sign-in-pictures/), [How to set the header picture, video, logo and tint](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/header-picture/), [How to write the words at the top of the home](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/write-header/), [How to write a page in several languages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-in-other-languages/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/).

## Stage 1: Know what you set, and for which event

App Studio keeps each setting at one level: for one event, for one language, or once for the whole app. In an app with several events, you pick the event first and set it again for every event that needs it.

Sources: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/).

### 1. Decide what each event needs: colours, fonts, the home screen and pages are set event by event.

- Who: Your move
- Set for each event: Colours, light and dark, fonts, pictures and the missing-picture choice; The home screen's layout, top bar, header, blocks and badge pictures; Custom pages, each with its tabs and blocks.
- The same for every event: Designed home screen, on or off; The event picker's colours and fonts, in apps with several events; Your wording in Translations, kept per language.
- Why: Texts are kept per language too: the home's header and block texts, and each page's title, tab names, texts and HTML.
- Watch out: You will not find the design style, the tab bar style or whether people may pick their own style in App Studio: your ExpoPlatform team sets them, so ask your ExpoPlatform contact.
- Done when: You know what to set for each event, and what is the same for every event.
- Go deeper: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/)

## Stage 2: Set each event's colours and fonts

Each event has its own colours, fonts and pictures, with a light colour set and a separate dark one. Colours & fonts and Name, icon & splash share one draft and one Save changes.

Sources: [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/).

### 2. In Colours & fonts, keep Light on and set the event's brand colours, or start from a palette.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Colours & fonts: Start from a palette, then the Brand colours, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: A palette fills all 21 colours of the scheme you are on, as a one-time fill you then adjust.
- Watch out: With several events, pick the event in the Colours header first: each event has its own colours.
- Watch out: Each colour must be a #RRGGBB code, or nothing is saved when you press Save changes.
- Done when: Each colour you set shows Use default under it.
- Go deeper: [How to change an event's colours](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-event-colours/), [How to start from a ready-made palette](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/start-from-palette/), [How to fix a hard-to-read brand colour](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fix-contrast/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/)

### 3. Press Dark at the top of Colours, and set only the colours that should differ in dark mode.

- Who: Your move
- On screen: “In dark mode, a colour you leave empty uses its light one.”
- Why: The phone preview turns dark with it, and each empty field shows the light colour it falls back to.
- Watch out: A palette fills the scheme you are on: press Dark first to fill the dark set with one.
- Done when: With Dark on, the phone preview is dark too, and the fields hold your dark colours.
- Go deeper: [How to set the dark-mode colours](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/set-dark-colours/), [How to start from a ready-made palette](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/start-from-palette/)

### 4. Choose the fonts under Fonts and the pictures under Pictures, then press Save changes.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Fonts: Headings and Body text, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: One Save changes saves the whole draft: every event's colours, fonts and pictures, and Name, icon & splash too.
- Watch out: In an app with several events, colour the screen where people choose an event in the Event picker block, above Colours: it is one set for the whole app.
- Watch out: A picture is stored as soon as you upload it, but the app uses it only after Save changes.
- Done when: You pressed Save changes, and a green message confirmed it.
- Go deeper: [How to change the fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-fonts/), [How to change the sign-in pictures and the loading backdrop](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-sign-in-pictures/), [How to choose what lists show when a logo or photo is missing](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/missing-pictures/), [How to go back to a default](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/back-to-default/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/), [Colours & fonts: field reference](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fields/), [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/), [How to colour the event picker (apps with several events)](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/colour-event-picker/)

## Stage 3: Design each event's home screen

Each event has its own home, saved with Save home screen, which does not save Colours & fonts. The phone preview runs it with your unsaved edits, on sample content.

Sources: [Home screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/).

### 5. In Home screen, switch Designed home screen on, and pick the event if your app has several.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Designed home screen, at the top of Home screen, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: It is one switch for the whole app: “When off, the app opens on its first menu screen and this design is not used.”
- Watch out: A new app starts with this switch off, unless it was copied from an app where it is on: check it before you design.
- Done when: Designed home screen is on, and the phone preview, started on Home, opens on the designed home.
- Go deeper: [How to turn the designed home screen on or off](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/designed-home-on-off/), [How to edit another event's home (apps with several events)](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/other-event/), [How to show or copy the event's original home page](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/original-home/), [Home screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/), [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/)

### 6. Choose the event's Layout, then what the Top bar shows: its top-right button, the search and the event name.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Layout, below the Home screen block, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: Layouts carry no colours of their own: “Colours come from Colours & fonts.”
- Watch out: An AI assistant button in the top right appears only once the assistant is ready for this event: follow Preparation on the AI Synapsium block.
- Watch out: Whether the AI assistant is switched on for your app at all is your ExpoPlatform team's choice: ask your ExpoPlatform contact.
- Done when: The phone preview shows the layout and the top bar you chose.
- Go deeper: [How to choose the layout](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/choose-layout/), [How to set the top bar](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/set-top-bar/), [How to add the AI assistant and prepare it](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/ai-assistant/), [Home screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/)

### 7. Under Header, write the line above the title and the title, pressing each language button you need.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Header: Line above the title and Title, with a button for each language, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: A text you leave empty uses the default: “Welcome, [name]” above the title, and “Your Digital Future Starts Here.” as the title.
- Watch out: A line written in DE, FR, ES, IT or RU needs an English text too, or the save is refused.
- Done when: A dot marks each language that has a text.
- Go deeper: [How to write the words at the top of the home](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/write-header/)

### 8. Under Header picture, add a picture or a video and your logo, and set the tint over the picture or video.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Header picture: Picture (4:3) and Video, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: The tint lies over the picture or video and keeps the words readable.
- Watch out: Tint over the picture stays greyed until there is a picture or a video: add one first.
- Watch out: Pictures and the video are stored as soon as you upload them, and the home uses them after Save home screen.
- Done when: Picture (4:3) or Video shows yours, with Replace and Use default under it.
- Go deeper: [How to set the header picture, video, logo and tint](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/header-picture/), [How to set the badge pictures](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/badge-pictures/)

### 9. Add, order and switch the home's blocks, choose who sees each, then press Save home screen.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: Blocks: each row has a handle, a switch, a chevron and a bin, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: Save home screen saves in one go the home of every event you edited since the last save.
- Watch out: The bin takes a block off the list at once, without asking: to hide a block and keep its settings, switch it off instead.
- Watch out: Restore the default home is saved straight away, without Save home screen: “Every change to this home will be lost.”
- Watch out: To give people a panel on their phone's own home screen, use Home-screen widgets, not blocks: switching a widget on or off needs a new build.
- Done when: App Studio says “Content saved — live on the next app fetch”.
- Go deeper: [How to add, order, hide and remove blocks](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/manage-blocks/), [How to choose who sees a block](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/block-audience/), [How to make a button, tile or picture open a screen or a web page](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/link-buttons/), [Home screen blocks and home-screen widgets](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home-blocks-vs-widgets/), [How to edit another event's home (apps with several events)](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/other-event/), [How to put the home back to the default](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/restore-default/)

## Stage 4: Add pages of your own

A page you write in Custom pages belongs to one event, with up to 10 tabs and 60 blocks, and an event holds at most 30 pages. A saved page needs no new build.

Sources: [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/).

### 10. In Custom pages, press New page, then Write a page, and give the page a title and blocks.

- Who: Your move
- On screen (drawn from App Studio's code): New page, Write a page, Add a built-in screen
- Why: Besides Text, a page takes most of the Home screen's blocks, with the same settings.
- Watch out: Each page belongs to one event: with several events, pick the event beside Your pages first.
- Watch out: What you write stays in this browser tab until Save page, and closing the tab loses it.
- Watch out: Who sees it decides what the app shows but does not protect the page: “everything on a page can be read by anyone who has your app.”
- Done when: The save bar lists nothing to fix.
- Go deeper: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [How to split a page into tabs](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/split-into-tabs/), [How to add a link in a Text block](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/link-from-text/), [How to show a block only to guests or only to signed-in people](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/limit-a-block/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/)

### 11. Under Language you are writing in, press another language, and write the title, tab names and text in it.

- Who: Your move
- On screen (drawn from App Studio's code): Title (English), Language you are writing in (EN, DE, FR, ES, IT, RU), Add to the menu when saved (on)
- Why: As App Studio's hint says, “English shows wherever a language is left empty.”
- Watch out: The title, and any tab you name, need English, and every Text block English text or a picture: write the page in English first.
- Watch out: Your own HTML is written per language, so write it, links included, in each language.
- Done when: In each language you need, no Text block's row says it has no text in that language.
- Go deeper: [How to write a page in several languages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-in-other-languages/), [How to open a screen or a page from your own HTML](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/link-from-html/), [How to split a page into tabs](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/split-into-tabs/), [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/)

### 12. Press Save page, then arrange the page's place in the menu in Menu & content.

- Who: Your move
- On screen: “Live on the next app refresh. No new build needed.”
- Why: With Add to the menu when saved on, a new page goes to the end of the Event section of the event's menu.
- Watch out: If Menu & content is already open in another tab, reload it before you save there, or its save takes the page out of the menu again.
- Watch out: A page saved with Add to the menu when saved off is listed under Not in the menu: press Add to menu there.
- Done when: App Studio confirms with the page's English title, then “added to Event — live on the next app refresh”, or “saved — it is not in the menu yet” with Add to the menu when saved off.
- Go deeper: [How to put a saved page in the menu](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/add-a-page-to-the-menu/), [How to change a page made before App Studio](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/edit-an-event-page/), [How to delete a page](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/delete-a-page/), [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/)

## Stage 5: Put the app's texts in your words

Translations holds the app's own texts. Your wording replaces the standard text, language by language, for every event of the app.

Sources: [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/).

### 13. In Translations, choose the Language, find the text, type your wording beside it, and save.

- Who: Your move
- On screen: “Change any text the app shows, in each language. Leave a field empty to keep the standard text. No new build is needed.”
- Why: No build is started: “Installed apps pick up saved texts within about 30 minutes.”
- Watch out: Sign-in and registration button texts and menu names are not here: word them per event in Sign-in & registration and Menu & content.
- Watch out: Language is locked while you have unsaved changes: save them, or undo them, before you switch.
- Done when: App Studio says “Saved. Installed apps pick the texts up within about 30 minutes.”
- Go deeper: [How to change a text the app shows](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/change-a-text/), [How to go back to the standard text](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/back-to-standard/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/), [How to work on another language](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/another-language/)

### 14. Tick Changed only to list the texts that carry your wording, and check each language you offer.

- Who: Your move
- On screen (drawn from App Studio's code): Language (English (en)), Search: sign in, agenda, my badge…, Changed only (ticked)
- Why: Your wording is kept per language, so each language is checked and changed on its own.
- Watch out: If Language does not list a language you need, ask your ExpoPlatform contact: your ExpoPlatform team switches languages on.
- Watch out: A text a language lacks reads “No standard text”, and the app shows the English text there unless you type your own wording.
- Done when: The count line under the search box says how many texts carry your wording.
- Go deeper: [How to see which texts carry your wording](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/see-your-wording/), [How to work on another language](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/another-language/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/)

## Stage 6: Check it, and see it reach phones

The phone preview runs the app with your unsaved settings, on sample content. Once saved, your look reaches installed apps with no new build.

Sources: [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/), [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/).

### 15. In the phone preview, check each event in light and dark, and, on Home, as a signed-in person and a guest.

- Who: Your move
- Picture: The Preview strip below the form: Signed-in preview or Guest preview, a screenshot of App Studio
- Why: With several events, the preview follows the event you pick in Home screen or Custom pages.
- Watch out: App Studio checks only Tertiary on Primary, so check that every other button and text stays readable, in both themes.
- Watch out: After Use default, the preview keeps showing the saved colour, font or picture until you save.
- Watch out: Translations has no preview: installed apps show your saved wording within about 30 minutes.
- Done when: Each event reads well in light and dark, for guests and for signed-in people.
- Go deeper: [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/), [How to edit another event's home (apps with several events)](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/other-event/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/), [How to fix a hard-to-read brand colour](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fix-contrast/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/)

### 16. Keep the look up to date: what you save reaches installed apps with no new build.

- Who: Your move
- At the next refresh: Colours, fonts and pictures, after Save changes; The home screen, after Save home screen; Custom pages, after Save page.
- Within about 30 minutes: Your wording, after the save button in Translations; The app checks for new texts at most once every 30 minutes.
- Why: Most sections say it in their save bar: “Changes reach the app the next time it refreshes.”
- Watch out: Save changes also saves Name, icon & splash: its icon, the name under the icon (in store builds, the store name) and the splash picture and colour reach phones only after the next build is installed.
- Done when: You know when each change reaches installed apps: once a build of this app is installed, it shows your look after its next refresh, and your wording within about 30 minutes.
- Go deeper: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/), [Name, icon & splash](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-identity/), [Colours & fonts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/)

## Something's wrong?

- **“Text on this colour is hard to read ([ratio]:1).” shows under Primary.** Why: The Tertiary colour on your Primary colour has a contrast below 4.5:1. What to do: Press the button beside it, which reads Use and a darker colour's code, or change Primary or Tertiary; the note is advice, and saving is not blocked. See: [How to fix a hard-to-read brand colour](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fix-contrast/).
- **After Save changes, a red message says a colour must be a #RRGGBB code.** Why: A colour is not a #RRGGBB code, so nothing was saved. What to do: Correct it to # followed by six characters from 0 to 9 and a to f, then press Save changes again. See: [How to change an event's colours](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-event-colours/).
- **The phone preview does not open on my designed home.** Why: Designed home screen is off (“When off, the app opens on its first menu screen and this design is not used.”), or the Preview strip below the form starts the preview on My Badge or Boot flow. What to do: Switch Designed home screen on and press Save home screen, or choose Home in the Preview strip. See: [How to turn the designed home screen on or off](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/designed-home-on-off/), [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/).
- **After Save home screen, a red message shows, and nothing is saved.** Why: A block's settings break its rules, a header Text colour is not a #RRGGBB code, or a header line in another language has no English text. What to do: Correct what the message names, then press Save home screen again. See: [How to add, order, hide and remove blocks](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/manage-blocks/), [How to write the words at the top of the home](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/write-header/).
- **Save page is off, with lines listed above it.** Why: The page breaks a rule, such as a title or a Text block without English: “Fix what is listed above to save.” What to do: Fix each line listed, for example an English title of 1 to 40 characters, and English text or a picture in each Text block. See: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [How to write a page in several languages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-in-other-languages/).
- **Saving a page says “This page was changed by someone else — reload it before saving”.** Why: Someone else, such as your ExpoPlatform team, saved the page after you opened it. What to do: Copy any text you want to keep, reload the page, open your page again and make your changes again. See: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/).
- **My new page is not in the menu.** Why: It was saved with Add to the menu when saved off, or a save in Menu & content, open in another tab, took it out. What to do: Press Add to menu under Not in the menu; if the Event section is full, make room there, in Menu & content. See: [How to put a saved page in the menu](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/add-a-page-to-the-menu/), [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/).
- **Language is locked in Translations.** Why: You have unsaved changes: “Save your changes before switching the language”. What to do: Save your changes, or undo each one, then choose the language. See: [How to work on another language](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/another-language/).
- **My new wording does not show on phones yet.** Why: A phone with a build of this app installed picks up saved texts within about 30 minutes, and your wording is kept per language. What to do: Allow about 30 minutes, check on a phone with a build of this app installed, and set your wording in each language people use; if App Studio said texts could not be saved, press the save button again. See: [How to change a text the app shows](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/change-a-text/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/).
