Build the menu and content

From each event's menu in App Studio to the features and pages people find in the app.

The result

Each event's menu holds the features and pages each kind of person needs, named, ordered and shown to the right people, and installed builds of this app show it at their next refresh.

From the guide: Menu & content·When your changes reach the app·Your old App Builder settings

Who does what

  • Your moveYou build each event's menu in App Studio, in Menu & content: its items, their order, names and icons, the bottom bar and who sees each item.

From the guide: Menu & content

Before you start, have ready

  • Your app's events chosen in Events & updates: until then, Menu & content shows only a message asking you to choose them.
  • For each event, the features and pages each kind of person needs, and the items, four at most, that should be tabs of the bottom bar.
  • Names of up to 20 characters for items and section headers, in each language your app offers.
  • Any icons of your own: App Studio asks for “A square PNG, one colour on a transparent background, at least 48 px.”
  • Your own pages, written and saved in Custom pages, if you want them in the menu.
  • Files for a documents page: PDF, Word or Excel, of up to 20 MB each.
  • For a contacts page: each person's details, and photos as PNG or JPEG files of up to 10 MB.

Stage 1 of 6

Put your pages in, then open the menu

Each event of your app has its own menu, in four sections. Item names, section headers and page content are kept per language; everything else is one setting for all languages.

From the guide: Menu & content·Per event, per language or for the whole app

1

If you wrote pages in Custom pages, press Add to menu on each one listed under Not in the menu.

Your move

It goes to the end of the Event section at once, and installed builds of this app show it on their next refresh, with no new build.

Watch out
  • If Menu & content is already open in another tab, reload it before you save there, or its save takes the page out of the menu again.
  • In Menu & content, a page listed as “Page title” has no name yet: press Edit and name it, or the menu cannot be saved.

Done when: The page's row reads In the menu.

Go deeper: How to put a saved page in the menu·How to write a page of your own·Custom pages·Your old App Builder settings·Menu & content

2

Open Menu & content, pick the event in Event, and the language if your app has more than one.

Your move
Watch out
  • Switching the event or the language drops unsaved changes without asking: press Save changes first.
  • Language of the texts starts on English each time the section opens: pick your language before you edit its texts.
  • Change this app's menu here from now on: later changes in the old App Builder no longer reach it, and nothing you save here changes your old apps.

Done when: Menu & content shows that event's menu, with its names in that language.

Stage 2 of 6

Add the features and pages people need

Add each item to the section whose people need it. In the app, “Event (everyone)” is shown to everyone, signed in or not; “My account (participants)” to signed-in people except exhibitor company owners; “My company (team members)” to signed-in team members; and “My company (exhibitor)” to signed-in exhibitor company owners.

From the guide: Menu & content

3

In a section, choose a feature, A custom page or A documents page in Add to this section, and press Add.

Your move

The item goes at the end of the section with its own name and icon, seen by everyone who sees the section, and not in the bottom bar.

Watch out
  • Add to this section offers only the features switched on for the event and not already in the section.
  • Save in an item's window saves the whole menu at once, with every other unsaved change on the page.
  • If people already use this app, fill a new page and set Visible for in its window before you save.

Done when: The new item is the last row of its section, unsaved until you press Save in its window or Save changes.

Go deeper: How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section·How to add a built-in screen to the menu·How to limit who sees an item·Menu & content·When your changes reach the app

Stage 3 of 6

Fill in the menu's own pages

Which kinds of page you can add depends on what the event offers, and each is filled in its item's window, which keeps your changes only when you press Save. Its tabs, documents, contacts and networks are kept per language, so fill them in the language you picked when you opened Menu & content, while a web page address is one for all languages.

From the guide: Menu & content·How to choose the event and the language you edit·How to write a custom page·Glossary·Menu & content: field reference

4

Press Edit on a custom page, fill its tabs, or switch Open a web page instead on, and press Save.

Your move

A custom page holds your own tabs, with their names, titles, texts and backgrounds, or a web page that opens in their place.

Watch out
  • Web page address has no format check in App Studio, so check the address before you save.
  • Remove the tab removes a tab at once, without asking.
  • A page built with the old App Builder's blocks opens as “Text (HTML)” and stays HTML, to keep every block.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved”, and the item's window closes.

Go deeper: How to write a custom page·How to edit a page made before App Studio·Menu & content: field reference

5

Press Edit on a documents page, add each file with Add a document (PDF, Word, Excel), and press Save.

Your move

The page lists each document under the name in its box, which starts as the file's name without its extension.

Watch out
  • A file uploads as soon as you choose it, but joins the page only when you press Save.
  • Remove takes a document off the page at once, without asking.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved”, and the item's window closes.

Go deeper: How to add documents to a documents page·Menu & content: field reference

6

If the menu has a contacts page, press Edit on it, add each person with Add a contact, and press Save.

Your move
Watch out
  • Phone and Email are not checked for format, so check each one before you save.
  • Remove takes a contact off at once, without asking.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved”, and the item's window closes.

Go deeper: How to fill a contacts page·Menu & content: field reference

7

On a social media item, press Edit, tick the networks under Show these social networks, and press Save.

Your move

The item carries only the networks you tick: Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube or Pinterest.

Watch out
  • On a newly added item none is ticked, so it carries no network until you tick one.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved”, and the item's window closes.

Go deeper: How to choose the social networks of a social media item·Menu & content: field reference

Stage 4 of 6

Make things easy to find

Name and order each section's items, and choose up to four items of the event's whole menu as tabs of the app's bottom bar.

From the guide: Menu & content

8

Name each section in Section header, order its items with Move up and Move down, and press Save changes.

Your move

In the app's menu, the header is the section's title, and the section lists its items in your order.

Watch out
  • Items move only within their own section.
  • A section header you empty and save stays empty.

Done when: On a wide screen, the preview's menu shows your headers and order, and App Studio said “Saved”.

Go deeper: How to change the order of items in a section·How to rename a menu section·Menu & content·How to check the menu before saving

9

Press Edit on an item, change its Name in the menu and its Icon, and press Save.

Your move

The name is the item's title in the app's menu, and on its tab when it is in the bottom bar.

Watch out
  • Names are kept per language: rename the item in each language, and save before you switch.
  • An icon you upload must be a PNG: App Studio refuses a JPEG.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved”, and the item's window closes.

Go deeper: How to rename an item or change its icon·Menu & content: field reference

10

Switch Bottom bar on for up to four items, in the order you want the tabs, and press Save changes.

Your move

In the app, the items become tabs of the bottom bar, beside the menu button, for the people who can see each item.

Watch out
  • The tabs follow the order in which you switched them on, not the items' places: to reorder them, switch them off and on again.
  • A hidden item with Bottom bar on still takes one of the four places.

Done when: The line above the sections counts the places used, out of four, and App Studio said “Saved”.

Go deeper: How to put items in the bottom bar·Menu & content·How to hide or remove an item

Stage 5 of 6

Choose who sees each item

With nothing limited, everyone who sees a section sees its items. You can limit an item to some signed-in people, show a custom page to guests only, or hide an item.

From the guide: How to limit who sees an item·How to show a custom page to guests only·How to hide or remove an item

11

Press Edit on an item, tick who should see it under Visible for, and press Save.

Your move

The item then shows only to the signed-in people your ticks match.

Watch out
  • Once anything is ticked, people who are not signed in no longer see the item, even with All visitors ticked.
  • If you tick exhibitor choices, check the menu with View as set to Exhibitor (company owner) after you save.

Done when: The item's row reads “Limited to some people”.

Go deeper: How to limit who sees an item·How to check the menu before saving

12

In a custom page's window, switch Only for people who are not signed in on, and press Save.

Your move

Signed-in people no longer see the page in the app's menu; people who are not signed in do.

Watch out
  • The switch is offered only for custom pages in the “Event (everyone)” section; for a page from Custom pages, listed as “Your page”, Who sees it on its blocks there can show each block to guests only.

Done when: In the preview, on a wide screen, the page shows with View as set to Guest (not signed in), and not as a Visitor.

Go deeper: How to show a custom page to guests only·How to show a block only to guests or only to signed-in people·How to check the menu before saving·Menu & content

13

To keep an item in App Studio but out of the app, switch Hide this item on in its window and press Save.

Your move

The app then leaves the item out completely: no menu row, no tab, no tile.

Watch out
  • Remove from the menu does not ask first: once you save, the item is gone from this event's menu in every language, with its content.

Done when: The item's row reads “Hidden”.

Go deeper: How to hide or remove an item

Stage 6 of 6

Check and save

The phone beside the form shows your unsaved menu as the kind of person you choose. Only saved changes reach installed builds of this app, the next time they refresh, with no new build.

From the guide: How to check the menu before saving·When your changes reach the app·Your old App Builder settings

14

In the phone preview, pick each kind of person in View as, then open the menu and tap through it.

Your move

View as offers Guest (not signed in), Visitor, Exhibitor (company owner), Team member, Speaker and Moderator, and saves nothing.

Watch out
  • The preview shows only on wide screens.
  • A change made in an item's window shows in the preview only after you press Save there, which saves it at once.

Done when: The phone preview shows the menu you want for each kind of person.

Go deeper: How to check the menu before saving

15

Press Save changes, then add the texts in each other language, and build each other event's menu in Event.

Your move

Installed builds of this app show the saved menu the next time they refresh, with no new build.

Watch out
  • Your ExpoPlatform team can also change an event's menu: what they save shows in Menu & content, and what you save is what the app shows.
  • A document, a picture or an icon of your own is stored as soon as it is uploaded, even if the menu is never saved.
  • Your old apps never show what you save here: a phone shows it only once a build of this app is installed, a test build from Build & test first.

Done when: App Studio says “Saved” for each event's menu, in each language.

Something's wrong?

Pick what you see.

  • Why: Menu & content was already open in another tab, and its save took the page out of the menu again.

    What to do: Put the page back with Add to menu in Custom pages, then reload Menu & content before you save there.

    Go to step 1 ·How to put a saved page in the menu

  • Why: The app's event, or for an app with several events its event tags, is not chosen yet.

    What to do: Choose them in Events & updates and save, then pick an event in Event.

    Go to step 2 ·How to choose the event and the language you edit·Events & updates

  • Why: In Menu & content, Sign-in & registration and Events & updates, switching the event or the language drops unsaved changes without asking.

    What to do: Make the changes again, and save before you switch.

    Go to step 2 ·Per event, per language or for the whole app·How to choose the event and the language you edit·Sign-in & registration

  • Why: It offers only the features switched on for the event and not already in the section, and A custom page or A documents page only while the event has a free place of that kind.

    What to do: Look for the item in the section, perhaps marked “Hidden”; a feature not switched on for the event, or a kind of page with no free place, cannot be added here.

    Go to step 3 ·How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section·Glossary

  • Why: An item has no name, for example a page from Custom pages that Menu & content shows as “Page title”, so the menu was not saved.

    What to do: Press Edit on that item, give it a name, and save again.

    Go to step 9 ·Menu & content·How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section·How to rename an item or change its icon

  • Why: All four places are taken, a hidden item with Bottom bar on included, or more are on, for example items taken over from the old App Builder.

    What to do: Switch items off until four or fewer are on, then save; to add another tab, switch one off first.

    Go to step 10 ·How to put items in the bottom bar·How to hide or remove an item·Menu & content·Menu & content: field reference

  • Why: It is hidden, limited under Visible for, a custom page shown to guests only, or in a section those people do not see.

    What to do: Check its row for “Hidden” or “Limited to some people”, a custom page's window for Only for people who are not signed in, and the menu in the preview with View as.

    Go to step 11 ·How to limit who sees an item·How to hide or remove an item·Menu & content·How to check the menu before saving·How to show a custom page to guests only

  • Why: The preview shows only on wide screens.

    What to do: Open Menu & content on a wide screen.

    Go to step 14 ·How to check the menu before saving

  • Why: Installed builds of this app get saved changes the next time they refresh; an app made before App Studio never gets them, and a phone without a build of this app has nothing to refresh.

    What to do: Check that App Studio said “Saved”, then look on a test build of this app from Build & test after its next refresh.

    Go to step 15 ·When your changes reach the app·How to check the menu before saving·Your old App Builder settings·Add a new app
