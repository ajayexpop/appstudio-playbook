# Fix a problem

What App Studio's messages mean, and what to do when something does not work.

**The result:** You know what went wrong and what to do about it, and when to ask your ExpoPlatform team.

Sources: [Messages, word for word](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/messages/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).

_App Studio Playbook v1.0.1, built from the App Studio guide v1.1.0, checked against App Studio c1421d4 on 14 Oct 2026. Each step links to the App Studio guide pages it comes from._

## Who does what

- **Your move**: You read what App Studio says and fix what App Studio lets you change.
- **Our team is on it**: Your ExpoPlatform team looks after what App Studio leaves to them.

Sources: [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).

## Stage 1: Find out what is wrong

App Studio's own messages say what happened, and the guide lists them word for word, with where each one shows.

Sources: [Help](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/), [Messages, word for word](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/messages/).

### 1. Note what App Studio says, word for word, and where it says it.

- Who: Your move
- Why: The guide's search finds most messages by a few of their words.
- Done when: You have the message's words, or you know which step of a journey you are on.
- Go deeper: [Messages, word for word](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/messages/)

### 2. Pick what you see from the problems below.

- Who: Your move
- Why: Each problem says why it happens and what to do, and opens the journey step it belongs to.
- Done when: You found your problem, or none of them fits.
- Go deeper: [Help](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/), [Messages, word for word](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/messages/)

### 3. Not listed? Find the message on Messages, word for word in the guide.

- Who: Your move
- Why: It lists App Studio's messages by section, with where each one shows.
- Done when: You found the message and the section it shows in.
- Go deeper: [Messages, word for word](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/messages/), [Help](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/)

### 4. If a picture or a text was refused, check Limits, sizes and formats in the guide.

- Who: Your move
- Why: It lists every limit App Studio puts on a field, by section.
- Done when: You know the limit the field has.
- Go deeper: [Limits, sizes and formats](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/limits/)

### 5. If App Studio leaves it to your ExpoPlatform team, ask your ExpoPlatform contact.

- Who: Your move
- Why: Set by your ExpoPlatform team lists what App Studio does not let you change, by section.
- Done when: You asked your ExpoPlatform contact about the item, by its name in the guide.
- Go deeper: [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/)

## Something's wrong?


### In "Launch your event app" ([the journey](launch.md))

- **The build buttons are greyed out.** Why: ExpoPlatform has not finished setting the app up in the stores, or did not set it up. What to do: Read the note above the list: while the set-up runs, wait, and ask your ExpoPlatform contact if you need to know where it stands; if it says “ExpoPlatform did not set this app up in the stores”, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to build the app for a phone](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/builds/start-a-build/).
- **Publish says “Not in the stores yet”.** Why: The store set-up is not finished. What to do: Read the line under it: while your request waits, wait until the set-up is done; once ExpoPlatform is putting your app in the stores, ask your ExpoPlatform contact if this takes longer than a working day; if they did not set it up, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to check what is left before the app can go to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/check-what-is-left/).
- **My new icon, name, splash picture or widget does not show on phones.** Why: They reach phones only with a new build, and so does switching a widget on or off; in store builds, the name under the icon is the store name, not App name in the app. What to do: For the icon, the splash picture and its colour or a widget switch, make a new build, install it and publish it; for the name in store builds, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to change the app's name in the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-identity/change-name/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/), [Home-screen widgets](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/widgets/).
- **The phone preview does not open on my designed home.** Why: Designed home screen is off (“When off, the app opens on its first menu screen and this design is not used.”), or the Preview strip below the form starts the preview on My Badge or Boot flow. What to do: Switch Designed home screen on and press Save home screen, or choose Home in the Preview strip. See: [How to turn the designed home screen on or off](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/designed-home-on-off/), [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/).
- **A build has been running for a long time.** Why: It may have stopped: an amber note on its card says so. What to do: Stop it and start a new one. If your builds keep failing, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to follow a build, and stop it](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/builds/follow-or-stop/).
- **A choice in Send to testers is greyed out.** Why: The app is not in that store yet, there is no Store-signed build of that platform, another release of that platform is still moving (a store release included), or, on Android, an earlier release waits as a draft or the newest build is already on another Google Play track. What to do: Read the reason the dialog gives and do what it says; if new builds are not Store-signed, or a release waits as a draft in Google Play Console, ask your ExpoPlatform contact; while another release is still moving, wait until it has finished. See: [How to send a build to your testers](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/builds/send-to-testers/).
- **Submit to the stores is greyed out.** Why: A request is already waiting for ExpoPlatform. What to do: Wait for their decision; it shows on Releases. See: [How to ask ExpoPlatform to publish the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/ask-to-publish/).
- **A release is stuck at Ready.** Why: Store review still has something to sort out, such as a missing demo account, a demo account the check cannot sign in with, or a demo event that looks empty. What to do: Open Store review in Publish and fix each item marked Your move; items marked Our team are your ExpoPlatform team's. See: [From test build to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/journey/), [How to give the store reviewers a demo account](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/add-demo-account/).
- **A store turned the release down.** Why: The store found a problem; its card shows the store's message when it gave one. What to do: Fix it, build again if needed, and ask again with Submit to the stores: the new release goes through ExpoPlatform's approval and the store's review again. See: [From test build to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/journey/), [How to follow a release through the store's review](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/follow-review/).
- **The Overview says “Not in the stores yet”, but people can download the app.** Why: The Overview counts a store as live only from App Studio's own store record. What to do: Check the live version on Your apps. See: [Your app's Overview](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/overview/).

### In "Make the app look like your event" ([the journey](look.md))

- **“Text on this colour is hard to read ([ratio]:1).” shows under Primary.** Why: The Tertiary colour on your Primary colour has a contrast below 4.5:1. What to do: Press the button beside it, which reads Use and a darker colour's code, or change Primary or Tertiary; the note is advice, and saving is not blocked. See: [How to fix a hard-to-read brand colour](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fix-contrast/).
- **After Save changes, a red message says a colour must be a #RRGGBB code.** Why: A colour is not a #RRGGBB code, so nothing was saved. What to do: Correct it to # followed by six characters from 0 to 9 and a to f, then press Save changes again. See: [How to change an event's colours](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-event-colours/).
- **The phone preview does not open on my designed home.** Why: Designed home screen is off (“When off, the app opens on its first menu screen and this design is not used.”), or the Preview strip below the form starts the preview on My Badge or Boot flow. What to do: Switch Designed home screen on and press Save home screen, or choose Home in the Preview strip. See: [How to turn the designed home screen on or off](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/designed-home-on-off/), [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/).
- **After Save home screen, a red message shows, and nothing is saved.** Why: A block's settings break its rules, a header Text colour is not a #RRGGBB code, or a header line in another language has no English text. What to do: Correct what the message names, then press Save home screen again. See: [How to add, order, hide and remove blocks](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/manage-blocks/), [How to write the words at the top of the home](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/home/write-header/).
- **Save page is off, with lines listed above it.** Why: The page breaks a rule, such as a title or a Text block without English: “Fix what is listed above to save.” What to do: Fix each line listed, for example an English title of 1 to 40 characters, and English text or a picture in each Text block. See: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [How to write a page in several languages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-in-other-languages/).
- **Saving a page says “This page was changed by someone else — reload it before saving”.** Why: Someone else, such as your ExpoPlatform team, saved the page after you opened it. What to do: Copy any text you want to keep, reload the page, open your page again and make your changes again. See: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/).
- **My new page is not in the menu.** Why: It was saved with Add to the menu when saved off, or a save in Menu & content, open in another tab, took it out. What to do: Press Add to menu under Not in the menu; if the Event section is full, make room there, in Menu & content. See: [How to put a saved page in the menu](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/add-a-page-to-the-menu/), [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/).
- **Language is locked in Translations.** Why: You have unsaved changes: “Save your changes before switching the language”. What to do: Save your changes, or undo each one, then choose the language. See: [How to work on another language](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/another-language/).
- **My new wording does not show on phones yet.** Why: A phone with a build of this app installed picks up saved texts within about 30 minutes, and your wording is kept per language. What to do: Allow about 30 minutes, check on a phone with a build of this app installed, and set your wording in each language people use; if App Studio said texts could not be saved, press the save button again. See: [How to change a text the app shows](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/change-a-text/), [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/).

### In "Let people sign in and register" ([the journey](sign-in.md))

- **Sign-in & registration asks you to choose the events in Events & updates first.** Why: No event is chosen for the app yet, and these settings are kept per event. What to do: In Events & updates, choose the event or the event tags and save, then choose an event in Event. See: [How to choose how people sign in to an event](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/choose-sign-in-mode/), [Events & updates](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-general/).
- **Events & updates says “No event has a tag yet. Tag your events in your admin first.”** Why: Several events, by event tag is chosen, and no event in your ExpoPlatform admin has a tag yet. What to do: Tag your events in your ExpoPlatform admin, then come back. See: [How to make the app offer several events, chosen by event tag](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-general/choose-events-by-tag/).
- **My changes disappeared when I picked another event or language.** Why: In Menu & content, Sign-in & registration and Events & updates, switching the event or the language drops unsaved changes without asking. What to do: Make the changes again, and save before you switch. See: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [How to choose the event and the language you edit](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/choose-event-and-language/), [Sign-in & registration](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/).
- **A change I made under one language shows in every language.** Why: Only the button texts are kept per language; the sign-in mode, the addresses and the switches hold one value for every language. What to do: Set those once for each event, and write only the button texts in each language. See: [Sign-in & registration](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/).
- **The register button does not show on the sign-in screen.** Why: It shows only while Show the register button is on and there is a registration address to open. What to do: Switch it on and save; with Another website, fill in Registration website, and with The event's registration page, the event needs a registration page of its own to open. See: [How to let new visitors register from the sign-in screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/add-register-button/), [Sign-in & registration: field reference](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/fields/).
- **Saving says “This could not be saved - try again” or “Reopen App Studio from your admin”.** Why: The first means the save did not go through, and your edits stay in the form; the second means your App Studio session has ended. What to do: For the first, press Save changes again; for the second, open App Studio again from your ExpoPlatform admin and make the change again. See: [How to choose how people sign in to an event](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/choose-sign-in-mode/), [Open App Studio](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/open/).
- **There is no phone beside the form, or it shows “Loading the preview…” or “The app's config could not be loaded…”** Why: The preview shows only on wide screens and can stay loading when its data is refused; the second message means the preview could not get what it needs to start. What to do: Use a wider window; if it stays loading, wait a moment, then open the section again; for the second message, try again in a minute. See: [How to check the sign-in screen before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/check-preview/).
- **The sign-in screen still shows the old pictures.** Why: Sign-in & registration cannot change them, and its preview draws the event's stored ones; Colours & fonts sets them, and the app uses them only after Save changes there. What to do: In Colours & fonts, with the right event picked, set them under Pictures, press Save changes, and check them there with Boot flow. See: [How to check the sign-in screen before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/check-preview/), [How to change the sign-in pictures and the loading backdrop](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/change-sign-in-pictures/), [Colours & fonts: field reference](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/design/fields/).
- **Phones still show the old sign-in screen.** Why: Installed apps show saved changes the next time they refresh, and only saved changes reach them; your old apps never show them. What to do: Check that you saved each event and language you changed, and that the phone has a build of this app, such as a test build from Build & test; it then shows them at its next refresh. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to check the sign-in screen before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/check-preview/), [Sign-in & registration](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/), [Your old App Builder settings](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/old-app-builder/).

### In "Build the menu and content" ([the journey](menu.md))

- **A page I put in the menu from Custom pages is gone from it.** Why: Menu & content was already open in another tab, and its save took the page out of the menu again. What to do: Put the page back with Add to menu in Custom pages, then reload Menu & content before you save there. See: [How to put a saved page in the menu](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/add-a-page-to-the-menu/).
- **Menu & content only asks you to choose the event, or the event tags, in Events & updates.** Why: The app's event, or for an app with several events its event tags, is not chosen yet. What to do: Choose them in Events & updates and save, then pick an event in Event. See: [How to choose the event and the language you edit](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/choose-event-and-language/), [Events & updates](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-general/).
- **My changes disappeared when I picked another event or language.** Why: In Menu & content, Sign-in & registration and Events & updates, switching the event or the language drops unsaved changes without asking. What to do: Make the changes again, and save before you switch. See: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [How to choose the event and the language you edit](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/choose-event-and-language/), [Sign-in & registration](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/).
- **What I want to add is not in Add to this section.** Why: It offers only the features switched on for the event and not already in the section, and A custom page or A documents page only while the event has a free place of that kind. What to do: Look for the item in the section, perhaps marked “Hidden”; a feature not switched on for the event, or a kind of page with no free place, cannot be added here. See: [How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/add-item/), [Glossary](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/glossary/).
- **Saving the menu says “Every menu item needs a name”.** Why: An item has no name, for example a page from Custom pages that Menu & content shows as “Page title”, so the menu was not saved. What to do: Press Edit on that item, give it a name, and save again. See: [Menu & content](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/), [How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/add-item/), [How to rename an item or change its icon](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/rename-and-icon/).
- **The Bottom bar switches are greyed out, or App Studio says “At most 4 items fit in the bottom navigation”.** Why: All four places are taken, a hidden item with Bottom bar on included, or more are on, for example items taken over from the old App Builder. What to do: Switch items off until four or fewer are on, then save; to add another tab, switch one off first. See: [How to put items in the bottom bar](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/bottom-bar/), [How to hide or remove an item](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/hide-or-remove/), [Menu & content](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/), [Menu & content: field reference](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/fields/).
- **An item does not show for some people.** Why: It is hidden, limited under Visible for, a custom page shown to guests only, or in a section those people do not see. What to do: Check its row for “Hidden” or “Limited to some people”, a custom page's window for Only for people who are not signed in, and the menu in the preview with View as. See: [How to limit who sees an item](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/limit-audience/), [How to hide or remove an item](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/hide-or-remove/), [Menu & content](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/), [How to check the menu before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/check-in-preview/), [How to show a custom page to guests only](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/guests-only-page/).
- **There is no phone preview beside the menu.** Why: The preview shows only on wide screens. What to do: Open Menu & content on a wide screen. See: [How to check the menu before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/check-in-preview/).
- **A saved change does not show on phones yet.** Why: Installed builds of this app get saved changes the next time they refresh; an app made before App Studio never gets them, and a phone without a build of this app has nothing to refresh. What to do: Check that App Studio said “Saved”, then look on a test build of this app from Build & test after its next refresh. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to check the menu before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/check-in-preview/), [Your old App Builder settings](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/old-app-builder/), [Add a new app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/new-app/).

### In "Get people using the app" ([the journey](engage.md))

- **Ask the assistant warns that the AI assistant is not switched on.** Why: The app's AI assistant is set by your ExpoPlatform team, and without it this widget “would have nothing to open”. What to do: Ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to offer a home-screen widget in your app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/widgets/switch-on/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).
- **People cannot add a widget you switched on to their home screen.** Why: A widget ships with your next build: phones need a build made after you switched it on. What to do: Make a new build in Build & test and publish it as a new version; phones show the change once it is installed, and to have people on older versions asked, or made, to update, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to offer a home-screen widget in your app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/widgets/switch-on/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).
- **The phone preview does not show my wording.** Why: The preview beside the page always shows Play & Win with the app's default texts and voting open, whatever you set. What to do: Save your texts: as App Studio says, “Attendees get it the next time they open Play & Win.” See: [Play & Win](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/), [How to change the wording of a Play & Win screen](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/edit-texts/).
- **Attendees do not see Play & Win in the app.** Why: The app shows the Play & Win row only to signed-in people, only when the event's menu carries the row, and only while the campaign is on. What to do: Check that Play & Win is on and saved for that event, and that it is in that event's menu in Menu & content; if it is not, ask your ExpoPlatform contact; people see it only once they are signed in. See: [How to run Play & Win for an event](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/run-play-win/), [Play & Win](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/), [Set by your ExpoPlatform team](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/set-by-team/).
- **People cannot vote, in the app or on the kiosk.** Why: Voting is open during the event's own dates, or earlier while Test mode is on, and never once the campaign is finished; the kiosk also needs Play & Win itself on. What to do: Switch Play & Win on and save; to open voting before the event starts, switch Test mode on and save. See: [Play & Win](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/), [How to try Play & Win before the event starts](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/test-early/), [How to set up the stand iPad kiosk](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/set-up-kiosk/).
- **The iPad shows “This page is not available.” instead of the game.** Why: The link is wrong, the kiosk is off or not saved, or the app is archived; the page never says which. What to do: Check that Kiosk is on and saved, and that the iPad opened the current link: after New link, save and scan the new QR code. See: [How to set up the stand iPad kiosk](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/set-up-kiosk/), [How to replace the kiosk link](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/new-kiosk-link/).
- **“This email has already played.” shows on the kiosk.** Why: The typed e-mail already has an entry for this event, from the app or the kiosk. What to do: One e-mail plays once per event, in the app or on the kiosk: that e-mail's entry is already in Entries. See: [How to set up the stand iPad kiosk](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/set-up-kiosk/), [Play & Win](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/).
- **The iPad shows “Couldn't load Play & Win”.** Why: The page could not reach the server; it tries again every 15 seconds. What to do: Check the iPad's connection: as the page says, “Check the connection. The page tries again by itself.” See: [How to set up the stand iPad kiosk](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/set-up-kiosk/).
- **“Fix the highlighted fields before saving.” shows when you press Save Play & Win.** Why: The actual number or the Book a meeting link has a problem. What to do: Correct the highlighted field, then save: the actual number is a whole number, digits only, up to 999,999,999, and the link a full web address, or empty. See: [How to run Play & Win for an event](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/run-play-win/), [How to close voting and show the result](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/finish/), [How to set the game's picture and the Book a meeting link](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/play-win/picture-and-link/).

### In "Change content during the event" ([the journey](live.md))

- **App Studio says “Your session has expired - reopen App Studio from your admin” or “Reopen App Studio from your admin”.** Why: Your App Studio session has ended, or could not start, and a save refused for that reason is not carried out. What to do: Open App Studio again from your ExpoPlatform admin, then make the change again and save it. See: [Open App Studio](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/open/).
- **My changes disappeared when I picked another event or language.** Why: In Menu & content, Sign-in & registration and Events & updates, switching the event or the language drops unsaved changes without asking. What to do: Make the changes again, and save before you switch. See: [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [How to choose the event and the language you edit](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/choose-event-and-language/), [Sign-in & registration](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-sign-in/).
- **A renamed menu item keeps its old name in another language.** Why: Item names are kept per language, and you renamed it in one. What to do: Pick each language in Language of the texts, rename the item there too, and save before you switch. See: [How to rename an item or change its icon](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/rename-and-icon/), [How to choose the event and the language you edit](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/choose-event-and-language/).
- **Saving the menu says “Every menu item needs a name”.** Why: An item has no name, for example a page from Custom pages that Menu & content shows as “Page title”, so the menu was not saved. What to do: Press Edit on that item, give it a name, and save again. See: [Menu & content](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/), [How to add a feature, a custom page or a documents page to a section](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/add-item/), [How to rename an item or change its icon](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/rename-and-icon/).
- **The phone preview says “Live preview crashed” or “The app's config could not be loaded…”** Why: The app inside the preview stopped with an error, or the event server is not answering, so the preview cannot start. What to do: For “Live preview crashed”, press Retry; for the second message, try again in a minute. See: [Find your way around](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/find-your-way/), [How to check the menu before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/check-in-preview/).
- **Saving a page says “This page was changed by someone else — reload it before saving”.** Why: Someone else, such as your ExpoPlatform team, saved the page after you opened it. What to do: Copy any text you want to keep, reload the page, open your page again and make your changes again. See: [How to write a page of your own](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/write-a-page/), [Custom pages](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/pages/).
- **The text I want to change is not in Translations.** Why: Menu names and the sign-in and registration button texts are worded per event, in Menu & content and Sign-in & registration. What to do: Change them there; for any other text, search with other words or with the text's key. See: [Translations](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/), [How to change a text the app shows](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/translations/change-a-text/).
- **My change does not show on phones yet.** Why: A saved change shows at the next refresh, and a text within about 30 minutes, on a phone with a build of this app; a change you have not saved never reaches it, and your old apps never show what you save in Menu & content, Sign-in & registration or Events & updates. What to do: Check that the section shows your change for the right event and language, that you saved it, and that the phone has a build of this app; for a text, allow about 30 minutes. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to check the menu before saving](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-menu/check-in-preview/), [Per event, per language or for the whole app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/scope/), [Glossary](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/help/glossary/), [Your old App Builder settings](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/old-app-builder/).
- **My new icon, name, splash picture or widget does not show on phones.** Why: They reach phones only with a new build, and so does switching a widget on or off; in store builds, the name under the icon is the store name, not App name in the app. What to do: For the icon, the splash picture and its colour or a widget switch, make a new build, install it and publish it; for the name in store builds, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to change the app's name in the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-identity/change-name/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/), [Home-screen widgets](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/widgets/).

### In "Release a new version" ([the journey](update.md))

- **My new icon, name, splash picture or widget does not show on phones.** Why: They reach phones only with a new build, and so does switching a widget on or off; in store builds, the name under the icon is the store name, not App name in the app. What to do: For the icon, the splash picture and its colour or a widget switch, make a new build, install it and publish it; for the name in store builds, ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [When your changes reach the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/changes-reach-the-app/), [How to change the app's name in the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/build/app-identity/change-name/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/), [Home-screen widgets](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/look/widgets/).
- **A choice in Send to testers is greyed out.** Why: The app is not in that store yet, there is no Store-signed build of that platform, another release of that platform is still moving (a store release included), or, on Android, an earlier release waits as a draft or the newest build is already on another Google Play track. What to do: Read the reason the dialog gives and do what it says; if new builds are not Store-signed, or a release waits as a draft in Google Play Console, ask your ExpoPlatform contact; while another release is still moving, wait until it has finished. See: [How to send a build to your testers](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/builds/send-to-testers/).
- **A TestFlight send says “Failed to submit release”.** Why: The release was created but not sent; with a demo account saved in Publish, its demo event may not be ready for review, or its saved login cannot be used. What to do: Check the demo account under Store review in Publish, then press Send again: it continues the same release. See: [How to send a build to your testers](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/builds/send-to-testers/), [How to give the store reviewers a demo account](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/add-demo-account/).
- **A release is stuck at Ready.** Why: Store review still has something to sort out, such as a missing demo account, a demo account the check cannot sign in with, or a demo event that looks empty. What to do: Open Store review in Publish and fix each item marked Your move; items marked Our team are your ExpoPlatform team's. See: [From test build to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/journey/), [How to give the store reviewers a demo account](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/add-demo-account/).
- **Releases says “Approved, but the stores could not take it yet”.** Why: ExpoPlatform approved, but publishing could not start: each platform takes one release at a time, and another release of that platform, such as a test send, was still on its way. What to do: Nothing: ExpoPlatform is on it. See: [How to ask ExpoPlatform to publish the app](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/ask-to-publish/), [From test build to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/journey/).
- **A store turned the release down.** Why: The store found a problem; its card shows the store's message when it gave one. What to do: Fix it, build again if needed, and ask again with Submit to the stores: the new release goes through ExpoPlatform's approval and the store's review again. See: [From test build to the stores](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/journey/), [How to follow a release through the store's review](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/follow-review/).
- **The store approved the new version, but it is not reaching people.** Why: Later iPhone versions, and Android beyond its first 5% stage, wait for your ExpoPlatform team to start the rollout. What to do: Ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to follow a rollout](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/follow-rollout/), [Your apps](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/your-apps/).
- **What now says “The rollout is stopped”.** Why: The rollout was halted, and ExpoPlatform is looking at it. What to do: Ask your ExpoPlatform contact. See: [How to follow a rollout](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/release/publish/follow-rollout/).
- **People on older versions are not asked to update.** Why: App updates reads Off when no rule is set, and a rule for a version that is not fully live in that store yet waits until it is. What to do: Check App updates on your app's Overview, and ask your ExpoPlatform contact to set or change a rule. See: [Your app's Overview](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/start/overview/), [Update your app after launch](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/updating/).

### In "See how people use the app" ([the journey](measure.md))

- **A tab says “No activity in this period”.** Why: Nothing in the tab can be drawn for your period and filters. What to do: Press Show the last 90 days or Clear filters when offered; if neither shows, choose other dates in the calendar, up to 92 days. See: [How to get numbers on a tab that says 'No activity in this period'](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/empty-tab/).
- **A tab says “These numbers could not be loaded right now” instead of its cards.** Why: The tab could not be read. What to do: Press Try again. See: [How to see how much the app was used in a period](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/read-the-period/).
- **People is higher than Signed in and Devices suggest.** Why: Someone who used the app before signing in, and then signed in during the same period, counts once as the phone and once as the account. What to do: Nothing to fix: Signed in counts only accounts, and Devices counts installed copies of the app. See: [Analytics: Overview](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/overview/), [How the numbers are counted](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/how-numbers-are-counted/).
- **The numbers seem older than they should be.** Why: A tab keeps the numbers it read for the same filters until you press Refresh there, and Latest activity does not move on its own. What to do: Press Refresh on that tab, then read Latest activity again. See: [How to check how fresh the numbers are and read them again](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/check-freshness/).
- **The Devices tab ignores your filters.** Why: Devices reads each phone's latest check-in, so the dashboard's filters, Latest activity and Export do not apply to it. What to do: Use its own Search, Platform, App version and Signed in filters. See: [Analytics: Devices](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/devices/).
- **Some cards do not change when you choose a user type.** Why: Languages, Where people are, Top cities, Operating systems and Phones ignore the user type, and Data completeness and the people counts in the Event list always cover every user type. What to do: Nothing to fix: How the numbers are counted lists what each filter does not narrow. See: [How to narrow the numbers to one event, platform or user type](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/narrow/), [How the numbers are counted](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/how-numbers-are-counted/), [Analytics: Tech](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/tech/).
- **Export says “The report could not be made. Your data is safe; try again in a minute.”** Why: The export failed, and no file was made. What to do: Press Try again in a minute. See: [How to export a PDF report or CSV files](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/export/).
- **A link does not open your view for a colleague.** Why: App Studio opens only inside each person's own ExpoPlatform admin, so a link cannot open the view. What to do: Ask them to open Analytics from their own admin and choose the same tab and filters, or send them an export. See: [How to keep the view you are looking at](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/share-a-view/).
- **Export says “This report is no longer kept”.** Why: The file was kept for one hour and is gone. What to do: Press Try again and make the report again. See: [How to export a PDF report or CSV files](https://ajayexpop.github.io/appbuilder/after/analytics/export/).
